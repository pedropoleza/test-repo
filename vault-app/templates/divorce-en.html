This packet explains Latino USA Multi Services LLC's limited administrative role and collects factual information supplied by the client for an uncontested New Jersey divorce matter.

PART I — PROCESS INFORMATION & SERVICE TERMS

1. WHEN THIS SERVICE IS APPROPRIATE

This service is intended only for matters in which both spouses have independently decided to divorce and agree on all material issues. If a disagreement exists or develops regarding property, debt, support, custody, parenting time, child support, or any other legal right, Latino USA will not advise either party on how the issue should be resolved.

2. ADMINISTRATIVE PROCESS

Step 1 - Intake and Client-Provided Information: We collect factual information and documents from the client and provide administrative, typing, translation, copying, and filing-support services based solely on the client's written instructions.

Step 2 - Agreed Documents / Settlement Terms: Any settlement terms must be supplied by the parties themselves or by their attorney. Latino USA does not negotiate terms, decide what either spouse should receive, or give advice about whether an agreement is fair or legally sufficient.

Step 3 - Filing / Court Processing Support: Where permitted, we provide administrative assistance with submission of client-directed documents and help organize court notices and paperwork. Court acceptance, scheduling, requests for corrections, and entry of judgment are controlled by the New Jersey Judiciary.

A New Jersey uncontested divorce may in some circumstances be finalized 'on the papers' without a personal court appearance; however, the judge may still require an appearance or additional documents. No hearing-free result is guaranteed.

3. DOCUMENTS GENERALLY NEEDED

  • Valid government-issued photo identification for each party, when available.
  • Marriage certificate.
  • Any existing court orders involving either spouse or the children.
  • If there are children in common: birth certificates, Social Security information if required for the filing, health-insurance information, and copies of relevant custody/support orders.
  • Any written, fully agreed settlement terms or attorney-prepared Marital/Property Settlement Agreement, if applicable.

Additional services, translations, certified copies, service of process, amendments, rejected/resubmitted filings, or other third-party costs are not included unless stated in writing.

5. IMPORTANT — NO LEGAL REPRESENTATION OR LEGAL ADVICE

  • Latino USA Multi Services LLC is NOT a law firm. No attorney-client relationship is created with Latino USA.
  • Latino USA does not represent either spouse and does not provide legal advice, legal strategy, legal opinions, or advice about rights, settlement terms, custody, support, property division, debts, taxes, immigration consequences, retirement benefits, or whether a person should sign any document.
  • Our role is limited to administrative assistance, typing/transcription of client-provided information, translation, document handling, and attorney referral when requested. The client remains self-represented unless the client separately retains an attorney.
  • Either spouse may and should consult an independent attorney before signing documents if there is any question about legal rights or consequences.
  • If the matter becomes contested, involves disputed facts or terms, domestic violence/safety concerns, hidden assets, complex property/business interests, contested custody, or another issue requiring legal judgment, Latino USA may stop administrative services and refer the parties to attorneys.

6. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES, LIMITATIONS & ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

  • Client is solely responsible for the truth, accuracy, completeness, and legality of all information and instructions provided. Client must review all documents before signing or filing.
  • Latino USA does not independently investigate assets, debts, income, residency, service, marital history, court records, or statements made by either party.
  • No outcome, processing time, court acceptance, judgment date, waiver of appearance, or approval of any settlement is guaranteed.
  • Government and court deadlines remain the client's responsibility. Client must promptly provide notices received from the court and keep contact information current.
  • Fees paid for work already performed are earned for the administrative services provided. Court fees and third-party charges are subject to the rules of the recipient and may be nonrefundable.
  • Latino USA does not provide tax, financial, immigration, or benefits advice. Client should consult the appropriate licensed professional when those issues may be affected by divorce.
  • Information provided to Latino USA is not protected by attorney-client privilege. Latino USA will use reasonable care in handling client information, but the client should avoid providing unnecessary sensitive information.
